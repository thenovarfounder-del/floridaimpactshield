Impact Cabana Doors: The Pool Bath Door, Hurricane-Proofed

FIS
Florida Impact Shield Team
Updated July 2026 · Licensed CBC Contractor

Every Florida pool home knows the cabana door: the single glass door that lets swimmers into the pool bath without dripping through the house. It is also, in wind mitigation inspections across the state, one of the most commonly missed openings, an old aluminum-and-glass door quietly voiding the insurance credits the rest of the home earned. Florida Impact Shield installs impact-rated cabana doors that close that gap: laminated glass, reinforced out-swing frames, and finishes that stand up to pool chemistry and salt air.

Impact Cabana Doors from Florida Impact Shield: Hurricane-rated out-swing doors for pool baths, lanais, and garage-to-deck openings, completing your insurance envelope and qualifying for My Safe Florida Home grants.

The Overlooked Opening

Wind mitigation inspectors document every exterior opening, and insurers award full credits only when all of them are protected. Homes with brand-new impact windows routinely lose discounts to a decades-old cabana door, the smallest opening costing the biggest percentage. Replacing it is usually the cheapest line item in a whole-home protection plan and often the one with the fastest insurance payback, a pattern our wind mitigation guide explains.

Built for Poolside Duty

Impact cabana doors pair laminated glass, full-lite for light, half-lite or blind-embedded for privacy, with reinforced frames and multi-point locks, out-swinging so storm pressure seals the door tighter. Marine-grade hardware and factory finishes shrug off chlorine, salt, and Florida sun. HVHZ-approved units carry Miami-Dade NOAs for the strictest jurisdictions, and every installation is permitted and flashed correctly for the wet-zone exposure poolside doors live in.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a cabana door?

A cabana door is the single out-swinging glass door that connects a pool bath, lanai, or garage directly to the pool deck, a signature of Florida home design. Impact-rated cabana doors bring that opening up to hurricane code with laminated glass and reinforced frames.

How much does an impact cabana door cost in Florida?

Most impact cabana doors run $3,000 to $6,000 installed depending on glass configuration (full-lite, half-lite), size, and hardware. A free in-home estimate provides your exact price within 48 hours.

Why do cabana doors swing outward?

Out-swing doors seal tighter under wind pressure (the storm pushes the door into its frame) and keep the swing off your bathroom floor space. Florida code and practice favor out-swing for exposed exterior doors.

Do impact cabana doors count for insurance discounts?

Yes. Every exterior opening must be protected for full wind mitigation credits, and the cabana door is a commonly missed gap. Upgrading it to impact-rated completes your envelope for discounts of up to 35% on the windstorm portion, and it qualifies for My Safe Florida Home grant funding.
