Frequently asked questions

Do impact windows actually work in a hurricane?

Impact-rated windows are tested against large and small missile impact plus cyclic pressure loading. They can crack or spider under severe impact while remaining in the frame, which is the point: keeping the building envelope sealed prevents the internal pressurization that causes structural failure.

How long do impact windows last in Florida?

Quality impact windows are long-lived assets, though salt air, sun exposure and maintenance affect frame and seal life. Warranties vary by manufacturer and component, with glass, frame and hardware often covered differently.

Do impact windows help with noise and energy bills?

Yes on both counts. Laminated impact glass reduces sound transmission noticeably, and insulated impact units typically outperform the single-pane glass found in older Florida homes, which matters given year-round cooling loads.

Are impact windows better than shutters?

For insurance credit purposes, code-approved shutters covering every opening can qualify equally. The practical difference is deployment: impact glazing is always in place, while shutters must be installed before every storm and stored between them.
