CGI vs PGT Impact Windows: A Florida Homeowner's Comparison

CGI Windows & Doors and PGT Innovations are two of the most respected impact window manufacturers serving Florida. Both produce Miami-Dade Notice of Acceptance certified products engineered for the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone, and both are widely used in South Florida installations. Choosing between them comes down to specific product lines, aesthetics, warranty terms, and budget.

CGI Windows & Doors Overview

CGI is a Miami-based manufacturer known for premium impact products with strong performance in coastal and high-exposure applications. CGI's Sentinel and Estate lines are popular for their design pressure ratings and clean aesthetics. Being locally manufactured in South Florida, CGI products are engineered specifically for the region's demanding HVHZ requirements.

PGT Innovations Overview

PGT is one of the largest impact window manufacturers in the country, with its flagship WinGuard line widely recognized in Florida. PGT offers a broad range of products across price points, extensive size and configuration options, and a strong dealer network. Its scale often translates to competitive pricing and reliable product availability.

Which Should You Choose

For most Florida homeowners, both brands deliver excellent hurricane protection and qualify for the same insurance discounts and grant funding. CGI often appeals to those seeking premium South Florida-engineered products, while PGT's WinGuard offers broad availability and competitive pricing. The right choice depends on your budget, aesthetic preferences, and specific window configurations. A professional assessment can match the ideal product to your home, and either brand unlocks the same up-to-45% insurance savings and $10,000 grant eligibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are CGI or PGT impact windows better for Florida?▼
Both are excellent, Miami-Dade certified brands that provide top-tier hurricane protection and qualify for the same insurance discounts and grants. CGI is a premium South Florida manufacturer engineered specifically for HVHZ conditions, while PGT offers broad availability and competitive pricing through its WinGuard line. The better choice depends on your budget, design preferences, and window configurations.
Do both brands qualify for insurance discounts?▼
Yes. Both CGI and PGT produce certified impact-rated products that, when installed on all openings and documented with a wind mitigation inspection, qualify for the same wind mitigation credits from Florida insurers, typically 10 to 45 percent off the wind portion of your premium.
Which brand is more expensive?▼
Pricing varies by product line, size, and configuration rather than by brand alone. CGI's premium lines can carry higher price points, while PGT's scale often yields competitive pricing. The best way to compare is a quote for your specific home, since installation and configuration drive the total cost more than brand.
Do both brands qualify for the My Safe Florida Home grant?▼
Yes. The grant covers certified impact-resistant products regardless of manufacturer, as long as they meet program standards and are installed by an approved contractor. Both CGI and PGT products qualify, so you can choose based on performance, aesthetics, and price.
