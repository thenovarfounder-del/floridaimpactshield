Tamarac's Hurricane Exposure

Central Broward County's Tamarac sits well within reach of hurricane-force winds that travel inland from the Atlantic, where sustained gusts and debris pose a serious threat to older homes throughout the city. That exposure makes impact windows one of the smartest investments a Tamarac homeowner can make, protecting your family and property while lowering your insurance costs year after year. Unlike shutters or plywood, impact windows deliver permanent, always-on protection that requires no setup when a storm approaches.

Insurance Savings for Tamarac Homeowners

Florida homeowners who install impact windows can save up to 35% on their homeowners insurance premiums through wind mitigation credits. When you replace your old windows with impact-rated units, you qualify for a wind mitigation inspection that documents the upgrade, and those credits apply directly to the windstorm portion of your policy, which is the largest part of most Broward County premiums. Over the life of the windows, many homeowners recover a significant share of their investment through insurance savings alone. See our insurance savings guide for the full breakdown.
