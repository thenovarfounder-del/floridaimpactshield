Frequently asked questions

How much do impact windows cost in Florida?

Costs vary by city, home size, window count, frame material and product line. Most Florida projects are quoted per opening, and whole-home projects commonly run into five figures. Local permitting requirements and access difficulty also affect installed pricing, which is why itemized quotes from multiple installers are worth collecting.

Does my city require a permit for impact windows?

Yes. Every Florida municipality requires permits for window and door replacement, and inspections are part of the process. A licensed installer pulls the permit as part of the job. Permitted, inspected work is also what lets you document the improvement for insurance credits.

Do impact window prices differ between Florida cities?

Somewhat. Coastal and high-velocity hurricane zone areas often require higher-rated products, and labor and permitting costs vary by municipality. The larger cost drivers remain your window count, sizes and product selection.

How do I choose an installer in my city?

Verify the license and insurance, confirm they pull permits, ask for local references, and require itemized quotes covering every opening. Partial projects usually forfeit the insurance credit entirely, so confirm the quote is complete.
